# THIS SOURCE IS AN INTERVIEW (host and guest)
A line starting with "—" is a new speaker's turn. The host asks, the guest answers.
- A clip is the host's question plus the guest's WHOLE answer, or a whole answer alone when it
  stands without the question.
- Never end on a question: that is the host asking, and the answer is outside the clip.
- Never stop in the middle of an answer; never start in the middle of one.
